# CORBIN shot bench — hyper-specific image concepts (v1)

Rule: one image per 5-min chunk, homage to the words or contemplation tool.
Mix it up, no formula, nice flow. Always open on landscape (shot 1 banked).
Prompt cores only — full prompts add style prefix at generate time.

## Landscapes / establishers (openers, lora2)

1. **Hurqalya garden** — BANKED. The opener.
2. **Mount Qaf ring** — single impossible mountain ringed by cloud-sea, tiny
   birds circling its peak, black sky. (Exile/recurrence chapters.)
3. **Midnight sun over black sea** — pale disc on the horizon line at
   "midnight", still water mirror. (Pole/orientation passages.)
4. **Emerald valley** — thin green horizon thread under black heavens,
   lone cypress. (Visio smaragdina.)

## Thresholds / passages (lora1 or lora2)

5. **Averroes funeral road** — empty road with single bier silhouette,
   mourners as distant dots. (Ibn Arabi life-curve.)
6. **Pilgrim's boat** — tiny boat on vast black water under stars.
   (Pilgrim to the Orient.)
7. **Stair out of frame** — stone stair rising past the top edge, lantern
   on the third step. (Ascent/mi'raj grammar.)
8. **Door in the sky** — free-floating arched door, light seams around it.
   (Twofold dimension of beings.)

## Emblems (lora1, 30s draws)

9. **Compass rose, Andalusia–Iran** — one rose, two coastlines. (Topography.)
10. **Veil across stars** — translucent curtain, constellation behind. (Method.)
11. **Heliotrope + gold sun** — flower turning to small #D4A94E disc.
    (Prayer of the heliotrope — the single gold moment.)
12. **Empty throne** — throne silhouette under black heaven. (Pathetic god.)
13. **Two overlapping circles** — mirrored, one gold-rimmed. (Unio sympathetica.)
14. **Mihrab niche, empty** — arch framing darkness with one lamp.
    (Orthodoxy / prayer method.)
15. **Mamluk lamp close** — glass lamp, flame, calligraphy-free glow.
    (Light Verse / divine responses.)
16. **Girih star unfolding** — eight-point star mid-tessellation.
    (Recurrence of creation — also the INTRO asset.)
17. **Mirror reflecting stars, held by no one** — floating oval mirror.
    (Heart as subtile organ.)
18. **Well with rope going up** — stone well, rope rising out of frame.
    (Sophianic poem / descent-ascent.)

## Figures (veiled always, lora2)

19. **Green guide by water** — robed figure, face veiled, hand toward waves.
    (Disciple of Khidr.)
20. **Veiled woman at the well** — back view, moonlit. (Sophia in Mecca.)
21. **Sleeper at the horizon** — tiny resting figure, viewer surrogate.
    (Recurs; the channel's signature.)
22. **Ringed halos, empty center** — concentric flaming rings, nobody inside.
    (Hadith of the vision — presence without depiction.)

## Gardens / closes (lora2)

23. **Rose garden under moon** — walled roses, nightingale implied, no bird
    drawn. (Creative feminine.)
24. **Black pool mirror** — garden motif returns, everything doubled below.
    (Hour closer — mirror of shot 1.)
25. **Mystic Kaaba** — black cube draped, circumambulating light ring, no
    people. (Around the mystic Kaaba — finale material.)
