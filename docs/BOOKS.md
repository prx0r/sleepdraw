# BOOKS — the translation × film flywheel (v1, 2026-09-27)

Loop: public-domain source → Pāṭala translates/verifies → sleepdraw
illustrates (etched plates) → KDP illustrated ebook + sleep film →
film description funnels to book, book backmatter funnels to channel.

## Why illustrated

B&W interiors = cheapest KDP print tier. Our etchings (white-on-black source,
inverted to black-on-white for print) are plate illustrations that cost
nothing and justify premium pricing. No stock art, no licenses, house style.

## Division of labor

- **Pāṭala** (`/root/patalacheckpoints`): ingestion → translation per
  TRANSLATION_PROTOCOL → scholar-attested evals. Owns text correctness.
  Anti-theatre law applies: no book ships unverified.
- **sleepdraw**: etched plates (etched.py on art or refs) + sleep film per
  book (film machine: bible → flow → bench → board → chain).
- **KDP**: illustrated edition per book. Film description links book;
  book backmatter links channel + next book.

## Rules

- Never reprint a copyrighted translation. PD source or fresh Pāṭala
  translation only.
- Every plate credited: "etched by sleepdraw from [source]".
- One book = one film = one channel lock. No orphans.
