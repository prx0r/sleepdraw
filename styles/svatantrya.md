# SVATANTRYA — channel style reference (v1, 2026-09-27)

Flagship Sanskrit imprint: recognition philosophy (Utpaladeva, Abhinavagupta,
Ksemaraja). Precise, spare, absolute. The goated one.

## World

Kashmir at 3am: black Dal water, one mountain tooth, temple lamp still burning.
Nothing moves except recognition itself.

## Visual grammar

- Yantra precision: interlocking triangles, bindu cores, perfect symmetry.
  Geometry lives HERE (and only here + theurgy seals).
- Kashmir: ridgelines, still water doubling everything, deodar black cutouts.
- Minimal figures: meditator silhouette (back view), lamp-bearer, empty throne.
- Palette: white pencil on black + ONE ember accent (#c1440e) — bindu,
  lamp flames, triangle hearts. Nothing else warm.
- Absolute stillness: vast black always wins; every element earns its place.

## Prompt template

```
{precise subject}, perfect symmetry, Kashmir ridgeline behind, black still water
doubling it, one ember light, white pencil etching on deep black.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music

Tanpura drone + single temple bell minutes apart + night insects. No rhythm.

## Intro + logo

Triangles interlock around one ember bindu → "SVATANTRYA" → hold → fade.
8s, reusable.
