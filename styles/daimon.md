# DAIMON — channel style reference (v1, 2026-09-27)

Crowley + Hillman + every culture's double: the one who walks beside you.
Includes the Crowley lane (Spare/Harris/Thoth), cross-cultural twins, owls.

## World

Edwardian London fog meets the underworld crossroads at midnight. Everything
comes in twos. The guide is already standing next to you when the image opens.

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Austin Osman Spare**: automatic drawing, crisp phantasmagorical tangles,
  sigil-like condensations, monochrome line on dark. Our line teacher.
- **Frieda Harris Thoth**: projective geometry, hard color fields, art-deco
  occult architecture. Our structure teacher (take the geometry, leave the
  palette).
- **Thoth/Hermes ibis**: ibis-headed scribe, Books of Thoth as stacked slabs.
- **Crossroads + twin**: two figures, one facing away; owls as escorts.
- Space: fog bands (horizontal holds = sleep-coded), doorways slightly ajar,
  mirrors that show the other one.
- Palette: white pencil on black + ONE violet-flame accent (#6b3fa0):
  third-eye dots, sigil cores, owl eyes.

## Prompt template

```
{twin encounter}, night crossroads, automatic-drawn tangles in the margins,
ibis or owl witness, fog bands, white pencil etching on deep black.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music

Low clarinet drones + night traffic far away + rain. Urban-occult hush.

## Intro + logo

Silver twin-discs overlapping (vesica) draw themselves → "DAIMON" thin
capitals → hold → fade. 8s, reusable.
