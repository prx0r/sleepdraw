# 1CONSCIOUSNESS — channel style reference (v1, 2026-10-22, owner direction)

Experimental jazz, black and white. High-contrast, grainy, alive —
a thinking session at 2am, not a lecture. **Owner already has the
brand/logo assets — do NOT redesign them** (use provided files as-is).

## World

Monochrome lab-club: ink diagrams on void, cigarette-smoke light, hard
white shapes in deep black. The thesis is played like a solo — one idea,
one chorus.

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Black & white only** — no accent color anywhere (logged: house standard
  gold/red accents waived for this channel).
- High-contrast ink/charcoal diagram language: anatomical engraving line,
  chalkboard equation strokes, film grain over everything.
- Motifs: cell outline dividing, Markov blanket boxes, embryo flash,
  morphospace arrows, strobing flip-book of one figure changing.
- Cuts swing: hold–cut–hold (jazz phrasing), never smooth crossfades.
- Where goldrender packs are used (platinum-essays scenes): render/pass
  grayscale — geometry survives, color does not.
- Owner-provided logo/brand assets override anything below.

## Prompt template

```
{concept diagram}, pure black and white, thick ink line, heavy grain,
high-contrast anatomical engraving style, deep black void background,
white figure only. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words.
Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music (owner direction: experimental jazz)

Jazz-textured, sleep-safe reduction: upright bass held as drone (no walking
line), brush hiss bed, one muted horn note or sparse piano cluster minutes
apart — flavor of the jazz club, zero swing tempo. ($0/CC-PD sources or sub
app — needs owner ear-check before lock; flag if a real jazz bed is wanted.)

## Voice tie-in

Wonder lecturer (profiles.json `wonder`): clear, unhurried, precision not
awe — the straight man to the wild bed.
