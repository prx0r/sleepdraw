# FOLK & FABLE — channel style reference (v1, 2026-09-27)

Storybook etching: Grimm read slowly, drawn frame by frame. Proven by the
Bremen film (8:58, on the dash). Warmest channel in the house.

## World

Black-forest night: deep woods, cottage windows glowing, massive kind moon.
Storybook, never scary — wonder with the edges softened.

## Visual grammar

- Storybook etching: crosshatched woods, cottage + window glow, animal
  characters with human patience (musicians of Bremen: donkey dog cat cock).
- Big kind moon, always. Path through trees leading somewhere safe.
- Warmth without color: dense white linework = lamplight feel.
- Palette: white pencil on black + ONE hearth-gold accent (#D4A94E):
  window glow, lantern, moon rim. (Shares gold with Corbin — different worlds,
  same hour of night.)
- No text, no faces in close-up (figures mid-distance, storybook scale).

## Prompt template

```
{storybook scene}, deep black forest, one glowing cottage window, big kind moon,
animal characters unhurried, white pencil etching on deep black.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music

Music-box + rain + distant owl. The gentlest bed in the house.

## Intro + logo

An open storybook draws itself, one gold window glows → "FOLK & FABLE" →
hold → fade. 8s, reusable.
