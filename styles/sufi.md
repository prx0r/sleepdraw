# SUFI — channel style reference (v1, 2026-10-22, owner direction)

Storytelling, tangible, warm — the tradition itself made physical: a lamp,
a carpet, hammered brass, paper. NOT distant/mystical-cold; the qissa
storyteller's house at night. Audience sits *inside* the circle.

## World

Dark green courtyard room after sunset. One brass lamp actually lighting
wool, wood, tea-glass, paper. Light falls on things you could touch.
Everything warm-earthy against deep bottle green.

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Background: dark green** (brand ground, logged house-black exception
  like monk-white): deep bottle green `#103026`.
- **Persian miniature night gardens** (Safavid/Mughal schools, medieval —
  PD plates): flat warm color, lamp-lit courtyards, cypress, birds.
- **Islamic craft textures**: girih tile, hammered brass, woven carpet,
  marbled ebru paper, manuscript gilding — grain visible, hands implied.
- Light = warm gold `#C9A24B` (lamp, gilding, edge-rim on every shape).
- Red is reserved for the logo (below), never scattered elsewhere.
- Motifs: lamp-and-mirror, Attar's seven cities, whirling geometry softened
  into craft (tile, not vortex), open book on a reed mat.

## Prompt template

```
{scene}, warm lamplit courtyard interior, deep bottle-green ground, brass and
wool and paper textures, gold rim-light, Persian-miniature flattening,
tangible craft detail. No text, no letters, no numbers, no words.
Wide landscape, generous dark green margins.
```

## Logo + intro (owner-fixed)

- Ground: **dark green** `#103026`
- **THE** — ALL CAPS, small, **red** (vermilion `#C6362B`), centered high
- **SUFI** — large, **white** `#FFFFFF`, directly beneath
- Intro 8s: green field up → lamp glow blooms lower third → THE (red, small)
  fades in → SUFI (white, large) fades in beneath → hold → fade to black.
- Same lockup = thumbnail core, always identical placement.

## Music

Warm, rhythmless storytelling bed: low ney-tone drone + soft oud resonance
(sustained, not plucked runs) + faint tea-house room tone. No percussion,
no tempo — tradition warmth without pulse. (Law: procedural/$0 or sub app,
owner ear-check before lock.)

## Voice tie-in

Storyteller, not preacher: warm, tangible, unhurried — the reader sits in
the circle with you (profiles.json `sufi_reader` direction).
