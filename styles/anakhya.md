# ANAKHYA — channel style reference (v1, 2026-09-27)

Sanskrit Trika + Tantra: Abhinavagupta's Kashmir. Vibration becoming form.
Biggest corpus (169M tantra). Yantra geometry draws itself in white-on-black.

## World

Himalayan ridgelines under a black sky, temple courtyard at midnight, ash and
breath. Everything still except one thing vibrating.

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Rajasthan Tantric diagrams** (cf. Wikimedia Kundalini Tantra, 18th c.):
  sun + moon discs, serpent channel, chakra nodes, yoni/lingam geometry.
  Diagram-first: the picture IS the teaching.
- **Rajput/Tantric minimalism**: flat fields, hard symmetry, ash-white on dark.
- **Kashmir**: Dal-lake stillness, mountain teeth, diya lamp rows on water.
- Motifs: Sri-Yantra triangles interlocking, serpent ascent, sun-moon pair,
  mountain, diya flotilla, ash-covered abstract body (no face).
- Palette: white pencil on black + ONE ember accent (#c1440e): bindu dot,
  serpent eye, central triangle core, diya flames.

## Prompt template

```
{yantra or temple scene}, perfect symmetry, sun and moon discs, serpent channel,
Himalayan ridge behind, diya lamps on black water, white pencil etching on deep black.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music

Tanpura drone + distant temple bell (single strikes, minutes apart) + night
insects. No tabla, no rhythm at all.

## Intro + logo

Interlocking triangles draw themselves around one ember bindu → "ANAKHYA" →
hold → fade. 8s, reusable.
