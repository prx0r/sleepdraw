# ALCHEMY — channel style reference (v1, 2026-10-22, owner direction)

Traditional art, full stop. The picture book of the Great Work: period
woodcut, hand-colored engraving, manuscript illumination — nothing modern,
nothing 3D, nothing "motion-graphics".

## World

A Renaissance study at night: vellum page open, brass instruments, furnace
glowing through a doorway. The plate IS the window (house A001 emblem plate
is the exact world reference).

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Our own imported plates** first: 320 colored emblem engravings + 352
  collected plates (sources/alchemy/{emblems,images}) — the house owns the
  look already; thumbs and stills are cuts of these.
- **Period line traditions**: Ripley Scroll (15th c.), Atalanta Fugiens
  engravings (1617), Lambspring woodcuts, medieval manuscript illumination.
  Verify death years for any extra Commons pulls (docs/visuals.md).
- Colored woodcut / copperplate engraving language: hatched shading, flat
  hand-tint color, twin columns, starfield-and-sun-ray compositions.
- ONE accent: gold `#C19B48` — **exact match to the 17 goldrender packs**
  (packs may render scenes; treatment stays traditional-art).
- Motifs: green lion devouring the sun, ouroboros, twin king/queen,
  vessel + flame, the four operations as framed emblems.

## Prompt template

```
{alchemical operation}, hand-colored Renaissance woodcut plate, hatched
engraving lines, twin columns with starfield left and solar rays right,
flat traditional tint on parchment tone, gold `#C19B48` accents.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Logo + intro (owner-fixed)

- **Logo = the alchemical symbol for the Sun (Sol): circle with central
  dot ☉** — drawn in gold `#C19B48` on black. That's the whole mark.
- Intro 8s: black → gold circle draws itself (single stroke) → central dot
  ignites and blooms one soft glow → hold → fade. Optional wordmark under
  the symbol only if owner asks; symbol alone is default.

## Music

Furnace-room tradition: low furnace hum + one slow bell minutes apart +
faint crackle at distance. Rhythmless, warm-dark. ($0 procedural or sub;
owner ear-check before lock.)

## Voice tie-in

Furnace-room reader (profiles.json `alchemist`): low, unhurried,
image-holding — reads the plate aloud.
