# THEURGY — channel style reference (v1, 2026-09-27)

Late-antique rite: Iamblichus, Chaldean oracles, Hermetica. NOT the encounter
(that's Daimon) — the TECH: fire, vessels, statues waking, tokens of the gods.
Same night, other door.

## World

Alexandrian rooftop temple at the blue hour that never ends. Braziers,
star-vaults, stone gods mid-blink.

## Visual grammar (named sources)

- **Fayum solemnity**: frontal, symmetrical, held faces (half-veiled).
- **Hermetic vessels**: alembics, bowls catching starlight, animating statues
  with eyes half-open, synthemata tokens (geometric god-seals) floating.
- **Chaldean fire**: braziers in rows, smoke becoming stairways, star vault
  with tracked constellations.
- Motifs: statue waking, vessel catching light, token/seal diagrams, fire
  altar rows, star map bands, ibis (shared with Daimon — same night).
- Palette: white pencil on black + ONE cold starlight accent (#7fa8c9):
  vessel contents, statue eyes, token cores.

## Prompt template

```
{rite scene}, braziers and stone vessels, one statue with half-open eyes,
star-vault above with tracked constellations, floating geometric god-seals,
white pencil etching on deep black.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no words. Wide landscape, generous black margins.
```

## Music

Deep bronze bowls (long decay) + fire crackle + sub-bass hum. Ritual stillness.

## Intro + logo

Seven small flames ignite one by one in a circle → "THEURGY" → hold → fade.
8s, reusable.
