# FOLK & FABLE — channel style bible (v1, 2026-09-27)

Everything on this channel filters through here: art, pacing, characters.
Prompt-styles, not weights — freedom over determinism.

## World (the manner clause — keep it, swap subjects)

Base recipe (from sleepdraw art.py): `STYLES["pencil"]` prefix, then
`Subject: <scene>` + `<manner clause>, <excess clause>`. Per tradition:

| tradition | manner clause |
|---|---|
| Grimm / Bremen (default) | in the manner of Gustave Doré engravings, German romantic pen-and-ink |
| Kwaidan / Japan | in the manner of Hiroshige woodblocks, sumi ink on black |
| Arabian Nights | in the manner of Sultan Muhammad, paradisal excess, unity in multiplicity |
| African folklore | bold linocut, Adinkra geometry, night palette |
| Russian folklore | painted lubok folk woodblock, icon-gold restraint |

## Visual grammar

- **Space**: vast empty black always wins; ONE subject cluster; horizon low.
- **Cast persistence**: describe characters with fixed wording in EVERY scene
  prompt — "the same small donkey with long ears, an old grey hound, a black
  cat, and a proud rooster" — so they read as the same troupe.
- **Medium**: white pencil etching on black, moonlit. No color in v1.
- **Forbidden**: faces in close-up (3/4 or silhouette), clutter, text of any
  kind, daylight, modern objects.

## Shot grammar (audio-synced — times come from storyboard.json)

Scene still → techdou DRAW (~60s of strokes) → hold (SETTLE) → fade to black
(3–4s) → black hold (2.5s) → next scene. Cuts land on the audio's scene
transitions (bridge/swell marks); silence at the crow = black.

## Motifs

Forking roads, village spires, bare winter trees, moon (anchor), window
warmth = the only light, animal silhouettes.
