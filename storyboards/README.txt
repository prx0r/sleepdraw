Three pixel-registered storyboard prototypes. Stage 1 and 12 each reproduce the exact provided source art, only proportionally resized/positioned on the viewing canvas. Middle frames are contour-based artist action mock-ups, not model-generated exact artist stroke streams. Original rough lines are gradually erased and contours extracted from the final source are drawn in different priority orders. Staged colours are reveal masks from the original painting. For truly replayable physical acts, vectorize the contours and record eraser/pen/paint operations in the Sleepdraw pipeline. All images are generated for exploration; do not mistake visual continuity for actual stroke logs.
